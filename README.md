## Projects

### [Does a well-being program keep nurses from leaving?](https://christysanchez.github.io/projects/nurse-attrition/)

A hospital had to decide whether to keep funding its employee well-being program. Using a simple linear regression on 36 months of data, I found that nurse participation explains about two-thirds of the variation in nurse attrition (R² = 0.669, p < 0.001). About 1 fewer nurse out of 100 leaves for each 10 more who take part.

- **Tools:** Excel, Analysis ToolPak
- **Methods:** Simple linear regression, hypothesis testing
- **Deliverables:** Written report, slide deck, two-page infographic ([PDF](assets/projects/nurse-attrition/nurse-well-being-infographic.pdf))
- **Context:** Course case study, WGU C207
